Who we are

Knowing God, growing in Him, sharing Him.

Citywide Praise and Worship Church is a growing, family-oriented church committed to providing people with the life-changing message of the Gospel. It is a place where everyone is somebody.

We exist to glorify God by knowing God thoroughly, growing in Him consistently, and sharing Him passionately. Whether single or married, with children or without, Citywide Praise and Worship Church has a place for you.

We invite you to come and worship the Lord together with us.

Our mission

“Go and make disciples of all nations… teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you.”

Matthew 28:19–20

It is the responsibility and privilege of every believer, in the power of the Holy Spirit, to bear witness to Christ: His coming, His death for our sins, His resurrection, His ascension and His promised return. Witnessing is more than talking about Jesus. It is living in such a way that others want to know Him too.

Statement of faith

What we believe.

The Citywide Praise and Worship Belief Statement.

1The Bible

The Scriptures, both Old and New Testaments, are the inspired Word of God without error in the original writings, the complete revelation of His will for the salvation of men, and the divine and final authority for all Christian faith and life.

2God

There is one God, Creator of all things, infinitely perfect, and eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

3The Holy Spirit

The ministry of the Holy Spirit is to glorify the Lord Jesus Christ and, during this age, to convict men of sin, regenerate the believing sinner, and indwell, guide, instruct and empower the believer for godly living and service.

4Man

Man was created in the image of God but fell into sin and is therefore lost, and only through regeneration by the Holy Spirit can salvation and spiritual life be obtained.

5Salvation

The shed blood of Jesus Christ and His resurrection provide the only ground for justification and salvation for all who believe, and only such as receive Jesus Christ by faith are born of the Holy Spirit and thus become children of God.

6The Return of Jesus Christ

The personal, pre-millennial and imminent return of our Lord Jesus Christ is our hope and has a vital bearing on the personal life and service of the believer.

7Future Events

There will be a bodily resurrection of all the dead: of the believer to everlasting blessedness and joy with the Lord, and of the unbeliever to judgment and everlasting conscious punishment.

8The Church

The true Church is composed of all such persons who, through saving faith in Jesus Christ, have been regenerated by the Holy Spirit and are united together in the body of Christ, of which He is the Head.

9Baptism and Communion

Water baptism by immersion soon after accepting Christ as personal Saviour is a testimony of death to sin and resurrection to a new life. The Lord’s Supper is a memorial service setting forth in sacred and symbolic manner the death of the Lord Jesus Christ; all true believers, and only believers, should share in it.
