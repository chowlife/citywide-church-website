Who we are

A growing, family-oriented church

“We exist to glorify God by knowing God thoroughly, growing in Him consistently, and sharing Him passionately.”

Citywide Praise and Worship Church is committed to providing people with the life-changing message of the Gospel. It is a place where everyone is somebody. Whether single or married, with children or without, Citywide Praise and Worship Church has a place for you.

We are a church that is

  • GroundedRooted in the Word of God and prayer
  • RelevantMeeting people where they are
  • DiversifiedYoung and old from many denominational backgrounds
  • UnifiedPositive, enthusiastic people
  • Community-mindedCaring for our “next door” neighbours
  • Global-mindedSharing the love of Jesus with the whole world
  • BlessedProud of our heritage, our best days still ahead
  • GenerousA river for God's blessing instead of a reservoir
  • CaringA “hospital,” not a “hotel”
  • GrowingAlways room for one more
