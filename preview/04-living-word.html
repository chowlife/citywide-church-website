iiWho We Are

A growing, family-oriented church — a place for you.

Citywide Praise and Worship Church is a growing, family-oriented church committed to providing people with the life-changing message of the Gospel. It is a place where everyone is somebody.

We exist to glorify God by knowing God thoroughly, growing in Him consistently, and sharing Him passionately.

Whether single or married, with children or without, Citywide Praise and Worship Church has a place for you.
