Ministries

Growing together

There's a place for every age and every season of life at Citywide.

Women's Ministry

Helping women grow spiritually, build genuine sisterhood, and reflect the character of Christ — in spirit, soul and body.

  • Small groups for mentoring, accountability and Bible study
  • Monthly fellowships, seminars and special events
  • Luncheons, teas, retreats and our annual Women's Conference

Men's Ministry

Equipping men to lead with integrity at home, in the church and in the community.

  • Spiritual: putting God first — integrity, holiness, purity, forgiveness and growing through hardship
  • Family: loving our wives sacrificially, raising our children well, and keeping God, family, ministry and work in the right order — including marriage, fathering, family finances and conflict resolution
  • Education: encouraging continued learning, with support for GED preparation, courses and connections to local institutions

Children's Ministry

Children are an important part of our church family. We help kids grow in character, respect and integrity through Bible teaching connected to everyday life.

  • Introducing children to Jesus through prayer and a firm foundation in Scripture
  • Building good values and moral strength
  • Learning about people and nations around the world, and how to share God's love with them

Prayer & Bible Study

We are a caring community: we teach the Scriptures, share testimonies of what God has done, and visit and pray with families in their homes. Midweek gatherings are open to everyone.
