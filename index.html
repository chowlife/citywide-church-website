Who we are

A growing, family-oriented church.

Citywide Praise and Worship Church is a growing, family-oriented church committed to providing people with the life-changing message of the Gospel. It is a place where everyone is somebody.

We exist to glorify God by knowing God thoroughly, growing in Him consistently, and sharing Him passionately. Whether single or married, with children or without, Citywide Praise and Worship Church has a place for you.

About & beliefs

Our values

We are a church that is…

  • Grounded

    Rooted in the Word of God and prayer.

  • Relevant

    Meeting people where they are.

  • Diversified

    Young and old, from many denominational backgrounds.

  • Unified

    Positive, enthusiastic people.

  • Community-minded

    Caring for our “next door” neighbours.

  • Global-minded

    Sharing the love of Jesus with the whole world.

  • Blessed

    Proud of our heritage; our best days are still ahead.

  • Generous

    A river for God’s blessing instead of a reservoir.

  • Caring

    A “hospital,” not a “hotel.”

  • Growing

    There is always room for one more.
